# Privacy policy and terms — IMEI Guardian
Last updated: 7 November 2026.

IMEI Guardian is an independent consent-based device management tool. It is not a telecom operator, law-enforcement system, or government service.

## Data processed
Account name and email; password hash; device label and model; encrypted IMEI and keyed digest; GPS coordinates, accuracy, battery percentage and timestamps submitted by the linked Android companion app; security and activity events.

## Purpose and consent
The owner registers a device and must install and authorize the companion app before it can submit location or heartbeat data. The app must display a persistent notification while background location sharing is active and provide a way to stop sharing. Do not register or monitor a device unless you own it or have explicit authority and informed consent.

## IMEI and location limits
An IMEI is an identifier. This service cannot query mobile networks, carrier towers, or CEIR to determine live location or online status. Location is available only when the authorized app has permission, location services, power and network connectivity. Last known coordinates may be stale.

## Security and retention
Passwords are hashed. IMEI values are encrypted at rest and also stored as keyed digests for duplicate detection. Location data is private to the account owner. Use HTTPS and protect credentials. Users can unlink a device or delete their account. Account deletion cascades to associated records.

## India recovery
For official lost/stolen handset reporting and IMEI blocking, use the Government of India's Sanchar Saathi / CEIR portal: https://www.sancharsaathi.gov.in/. This independent app has no direct CEIR integration and does not submit reports for you.
