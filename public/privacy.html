Privacy & terms

IMEI Guardian is an independent, consent-based device recovery service.

Data

We process account name/email, password hash, device metadata, encrypted IMEI, GPS updates voluntarily sent by the authorized Android app, and security events.

Consent

Only register and monitor a device you own or are authorized to manage. The companion app must request location permission and display an ongoing notification while background sharing is active. Users can stop sharing and unlink devices.

IMEI and GPS limitations

IMEI is an identifier, not a GPS transmitter. This service cannot query carrier towers, telecom databases or CEIR. A phone must be powered, connected, and running the authorized app with location permission to send new coordinates. Last-known location can be stale.

India lost phone reporting

Use the official Sanchar Saathi / CEIR portal for official lost/stolen handset reporting and IMEI blocking. This project has no direct CEIR integration.

Security and deletion

Passwords are hashed, IMEI is encrypted at rest, and location records are restricted to the owning account. Account deletion removes associated data. Use HTTPS and keep account credentials private.

Service is provided as-is and does not guarantee device recovery or location availability. Have these terms reviewed by qualified counsel before commercial launch.
